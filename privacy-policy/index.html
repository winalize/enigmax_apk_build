This Privacy Policy explains how eNigMax ("the App") handles information when you use the application. eNigMax is developed and published by Winalize.

eNigMax does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally request personal information such as your name, address, phone number, password, or financial information.

However, certain third-party services used by the App may automatically collect limited technical information necessary to provide their services. This may include:

Advertising providers may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and related technical information for purposes such as displaying advertisements, preventing fraud, measuring advertising performance, and complying with applicable legal requirements.

The collection and processing of information by Google services is subject to Google's own privacy policies.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

eNigMax uses an internet connection to retrieve information required for application functionality, including sports-related data, application configuration, updates, and advertising content.

The App may connect to remote configuration services to obtain application settings, feature configuration, compatibility information, and minimum supported application version information.

This functionality may be used to inform users when an application update is required.

eNigMax may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies, including services provided by Google.

These services may include:

eNigMax does not sell users' personal information.

Technical information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide advertising, application functionality, diagnostics, security, or related services.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect application information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

eNigMax is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information directly from children.

Depending on your device, Android version, region, and Google settings, you may be able to manage advertising personalization and application permissions through your Android device or Google account settings.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the App, third-party services, legal requirements, or data practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
